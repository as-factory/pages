FAQ

  • Q. Cannot save the signature I created
    A. Please change the app's permissions in the "Settings" app. Go to "Settings" - "Apps" - "nir sign" and allow access to "Photos".
  • Q. I cannot draw my signature smoothly
    A. Please move your finger slowly while drawing. Use redraw or erase as needed.
  • Q. Are created signature images sent outside the device?
    A. No. Created signature images are processed on your device and are never sent to external servers.
