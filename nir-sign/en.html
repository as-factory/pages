FAQ

  • Q. What can I use this app for?
    A. You can display entered messages in large scrolling text for cheering, events, live shows, sports viewing, store guidance, and more.
  • Q. Can I use it with just one iPhone?
    A. Yes. In single-device display mode, one iPhone can be used like a scrolling LED sign.
  • Q. Can multiple iPhones be connected?
    A. Yes. Nearby iPhones can work together so that each device displays one part of a large virtual screen, creating a wider display area.
  • Q. Is an internet connection required?
    A. No. Multi-device connection uses MultipeerConnectivity to connect nearby devices via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. No external server is required.
  • Q. Can I set the order of devices?
    A. Yes. When using multiple devices, you can set each device's position from left to right to adjust the display area.

Privacy Policy

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

The "nir sign" app does not collect, store, or transfer any personally identifiable information.

2. Handling of Entered Text and Display Data

Text entered in this app, display settings, and device order information are used for display and synchronization with nearby devices. These data are not sent to external servers.

3. Use of Device-to-Device Communication

This app may use nearby device communication through MultipeerConnectivity to connect multiple iPhones. Communication is performed between nearby devices and does not go through an external server.

4. Data Provision to Third Parties

This app does not sell or share acquired information with third parties.
