FAQ

  • Q. Compression takes too long
    A. It may take some time depending on the length and resolution of the video and your device's performance. Please wait for completion without closing the app.
  • Q. Quality is too low after compression
    A. You can adjust the compression rate from the settings screen. If you want to maintain higher quality, change the setting to "High Quality".
  • Q. Which video formats are supported?
    A. It supports formats standardly supported by iOS, such as MP4 and MOV.
