FAQ

  • Q. Cannot save to Camera Roll
    A. Please change the app's permissions in the "Settings" app. Go to "Settings" - "Privacy" - "Photos" and allow access for "nir cam".
  • Q. Camera does not start
    A. Please change the app's permissions in the "Settings" app. Go to "Settings" - "Privacy" - "Camera" and allow access for "nir cam".
  • Q. Cannot connect to thermal printer
    A. Please change the app's permissions in the "Settings" app. Go to "Settings" - "Bluetooth" and allow access for "nir cam". Also, ensure Bluetooth is turned on.
  • Q. Which thermal printers are supported?
    A. Currently, "YMP-01" is the only confirmed model. If you have other printers you'd like to use, please request them via the inquiry form.

Privacy Policy

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

The "nir cam" app does not collect, store, or transfer any personally identifiable information (name, contact information, device identifiers, etc.).

2. Use of Camera and Photo Library

This app uses the device's camera for real-time video processing and art expression generation. Data obtained from the camera is processed only within the device and is never sent to external servers. Images selected for saving by the user are stored in the device's photo library, and the developer cannot view their contents.

3. Data Provision to Third Parties

This app does not sell or share acquired information with third parties. We do not use external services such as advertising networks or analytics tools.
